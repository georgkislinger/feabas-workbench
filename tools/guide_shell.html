FEABAS Workbench user guide

Every window, every setting: what it does, when to touch it, and what it makes stale if you change it later. Written against the seven windows of the app, in the order the pipeline runs.

The workbench is a front end for FEABAS (Yuelong Wu and Jeff W. Lichtman, Harvard), which does the stitching and alignment; the method is described in Wu & Lichtman, 2026, bioRxiv – cite that paper for any result you publish.
